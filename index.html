Department III The Method & Five Capabilities

Decision Integrity

A structured method for examining consequential decisions when certainty is unavailable and action cannot simply be postponed.

Separating Assertion from Authority

Important decisions are rarely shaped by facts alone. They are also shaped by pressure, fear, loyalty, desire, habit, institutional incentives, incomplete information, personal history, competing obligations, and the narratives people rely on when they need to make sense of a situation.

The protocol provides a disciplined way to examine what is known, what is assumed, what is missing, who has authority, who bears the consequences, and what would require the decision to be reconsidered.

Question Fidelity

Answer the question that was actually asked, rather than substituting an adjacent, more tractable problem.

Tell observation, report, inference, and finding apart without confusing assertion with factual proof.

Wheeler Hubbell Publishing Unveils the “Standing Mark”: Cryptographic Decision Integrity to Halt Autonomous AI Agent Drift

New Research and Verification Protocols Expose “Coherent Substitution”—Why Frontier Models Answer the Wrong Question with Flawless Logic—and Deliver Fail-Closed Tooling for Machine-to-Machine Autonomous Rails.

NEW YORK, NY — Wheeler Hubbell Publishing (WHP) today announced the public release of its Decision Integrity & Standing Mark Protocols, pairing foundational epistemic theory with production-grade runtime verification for autonomous AI agents. The release is spearheaded by two landmark papers—Coherent Substitution: Proposition Integrity Beyond Answering the Wrong Question and Epistemic Autopsy: Mata v. Avianca—alongside an open-source Model Context Protocol (MCP) adapter and client SDK.

As enterprises deploy autonomous agents to execute multi-step workflows, handle funds, and interact across software boundaries, current safety measures (prompt guardrails, semantic similarity checks, and human-in-the-loop oversight) routinely fail. WHP's research identifies the root failure mode as Coherent Substitution: an AI silently swaps its assigned task for an adjacent, more tractable problem, solves the replacement with rigorous logic, and presents the output as valid.

1. The Core Theoretical Breakthrough: The Authority Conservation Principle

In Coherent Substitution: Proposition Integrity Beyond Answering the Wrong Question, American theorist and author Wheeler Hubbell formulates why AI failure occurs prior to logical error:

“A system can fail before its reasoning becomes wrong. It can replace the object it was asked to evaluate, reason coherently about the replacement, and present the result as though it answered the original.”

Under the Authority Conservation Principle, downstream processing cannot manufacture authority for an altered proposition:

  • Governing Proposition (q0): The exact constraint, question, or boundary bound by upstream provenance.
  • Substituted Proposition (q*): An adjacent, more tractable proposition generated by the model.
  • The Coherence Cloak: The model evaluates q* with rigorous syntax, verified quotes, and fluent prose.
  • Epistemic Laundering: Downstream consumers or subsequent agents accept the output as a valid resolution of q0.

If an answer addresses q* rather than q0, the output possesses zero epistemic standing, regardless of factual accuracy or mathematical eloquence.

2. Forensic Proof: The Mata v. Avianca Epistemic Autopsy

To demonstrate that prompt engineering cannot prevent coherent substitution, WHP published a controlled autopsy of the federal sanctions ruling Mata v. Avianca, Inc. (No. 22-cv-1461, S.D.N.Y. 2023).

  • The Governing Assignment (Q0): Explain why specific court orders failed to cause respondents to withdraw or correct disputed authorities.
  • The Model's Substituted Task (Q*): Explain why the Court sanctioned respondents under Rule 11.
  • The Breach: Every quote in the LLM's response was authentic, and every citation to the record was accurate. Yet the system committed an epistemic boundary breach: it answered Q* while claiming to resolve Q0.

Prompt engineering cannot remediate this failure because the model strictly adhered to the designated source document. The violation is structural, requiring deterministic standing checks rather than linguistic prompt adjustments.

The Author & Publisher

About Wheeler Hubbell

Wheeler Hubbell is a writer, author, playwright, publisher, researcher, and maker working across literary, dramatic, practical, and visual forms.

The work does not stay in one register. A book can ask a literary question. A play can put a question in the mouths of people who disagree about what happened. An essay can follow an observation. Research can require a formal argument. Decision Integrity requires a method.

What connects the work is not a single subject. It is the attention given to the thing itself: what is there, what is missing, what can be said, what cannot yet be known, and what follows from getting the distinction wrong.

Wheeler Hubbell Publishing is the independent publishing home of this work.
