Wheeler Hubbell Publishing Introduces The Standing Mark

An independent verification infrastructure for decision integrity and autonomous systems.

NEW YORK — November 11, 2026 — Wheeler Hubbell Publishing, Inc. announces The Standing Mark, an institutional verification service developed to establish and preserve the standing of claims, representations, and actions within defined technical and institutional contexts.

As autonomous systems assume increasingly consequential roles in communication, delegation, decision-making, and execution, questions of authority and accountability require more than identification or authentication alone. Systems must also be able to establish the conditions under which a claim may properly be relied upon and an action permitted to proceed.

The Standing Mark addresses this distinction.

Developed under the company’s Decision Integrity Protocol, The Standing Mark provides a structured means of evaluating claims of standing and issuing records that can be independently verified. Each determination is bounded by its stated scope, applicable conditions, and issuing authority.

The service is designed for use across machine-to-machine interactions, autonomous agent environments, and other settings in which the provenance, authority, and conditions of a claim must remain available for independent examination.

Decision Integrity

The Decision Integrity Protocol provides the underlying framework for examining how claims, authority, and decisions retain their integrity as they move between people, institutions, and technical systems.

It addresses a distinction frequently obscured in automated environments: authentication establishes the identity or integrity of a communication; authorization establishes permission; neither, by itself, establishes the standing of the underlying claim or decision.

The Standing Mark gives this distinction an operational expression.

An Infrastructure for Independent Verification

The Standing Mark is designed to operate as a machine-readable verification layer rather than as a discretionary endorsement or general assurance.

Its architecture supports the issuance, preservation, and independent examination of standing records. The resulting determination remains limited to the object and conditions actually assessed.

This approach permits receiving systems to examine the basis and scope of a determination without treating the originating system’s assertion as sufficient evidence in itself.

The service is intended to support integration into existing technical environments, including agent frameworks, orchestration systems, and other infrastructure through which autonomous systems communicate and act.

The Work Ahead

Wheeler Hubbell Publishing considers independent verification of standing an important component of the developing infrastructure for autonomous operations.

The company’s work is directed toward establishing practical requirements for verification in circumstances involving delegated authority, consequential decisions, and reliance between independent systems.

Further development will focus on interoperability, implementation, and the incorporation of decision-integrity requirements into the technical and institutional arrangements governing autonomous activity.

The Standing Mark is available through Wheeler Hubbell Publishing’s technical service infrastructure.
