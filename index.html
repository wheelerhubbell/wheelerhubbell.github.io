The Named Failures

Four structural ways a claim acquires force it has not earned during passage across humans or autonomous agents:

01 · Coherent Substitution

Reasoning well about a replaced question

The system replaces the governing proposition ($Q_0$) with a nearby, easier proposition ($Q_1$), reasons coherently about the substitute, and presents the result as responsive.

02 · Authority Leakage

Force exceeding the record

An informational object or relation exercises greater epistemic or operational force (such as EXECUTE) than its source, conditions, and warrant establish.

03 · Object Drift

Stable words, mutated object

The label remains continuous across handoffs while the underlying object, population, or version state silently changes underneath it.

04 · Qualifier Stripping

Binding conditions dropped

When limiting language (“in this sample,” “provisional pending review,” “opt-out setting”) disappears in compression, the surviving words claim an unearned scope.
