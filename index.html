Core Governing Engine

The Test of Passage & Diagnostic Invariants

Grounding autonomous reasoning in five continuous epistemic dimensions: Object, Provenance, Warrant, Boundary, and Standing.

The Test of Passage Transition Licensing

For every material transition in an automated or institutional workflow, the engine requires and records:

  • Prior Governed State: The baseline established condition and known verified facts.
  • Proposed Governed State: The consequential action, deployment, or state transition requested.
  • Material Difference: The exact Delta across epistemic and institutional boundaries.
  • Governing Warrant: The specific inspectable rule, evidence, or authority licensing the transition.
  • Maximum Permitted Result: Operation-specific force ceiling (Inquiry vs Action).
The Four Named Failures Diagnostic Taxonomy
  • Coherent Substitution: Reasoning smoothly about a materially different proposition while presenting the output as responsive.
  • Authority Leakage: Treating external, hypothetical, or caller-submitted assertions as verified institutional determinations.
  • Object Drift: Mutating the subject, claim, or condition across inference steps until action applies to a phantom reality.
  • Qualifier Stripping: Removing conditional boundaries, uncertainties, or jurisdictional limits before downstream invocation.
