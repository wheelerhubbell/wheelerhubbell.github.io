The Standing Mark

A signed record—not a substitute for judgment.

The Standing Mark Service applies the Decision Integrity framework to submitted material and returns an assessment for the requesting system to inspect within its own authority structure.

The examination is distinct from the decision or transaction that may follow.

A completed examination produces a cryptographically signed assessment record. When the applicable criteria are satisfied, that record may carry a positive Standing Mark. When they are not, the outcome remains a signed assessment without a positive Mark.

A negative or limited finding is an examination result, not a service failure.

What the signature establishes

A valid signature establishes that the record was signed by the holder of the identified signing key and that its signed contents have not been altered since signing.

What it does not establish

A signature does not establish that:

  • Every assertion in the submission is true.
  • Every source is authentic or the evidence is complete.
  • The examination is correct.
  • The governing text was faithfully applied.
  • The receiver has authority to carry out the proposed action.

Those remain matters for examination and verification.

A signed record preserves an assessment and its outcome. It does not convert that outcome into universal authorization.

Examination Is Not Endorsement

Payment buys an examination, never a favorable result.

The price is the same regardless of the findings.

A completed examination must preserve its actual outcome, including uncertainty, limitations, and findings that do not support the requested action. Payment does not purchase approval, and disagreement does not rewrite the record.

The person or system relying on an examination remains responsible for deciding whether it is sufficient for its purpose and whether it has the authority to act.

        Scope and Limits

        The service examines the material you supply. It does not automatically verify external facts, authenticate every source, or establish that evidence outside the submission is complete.

        An examination does not replace relevant legal, financial, medical, or other specialist advice.

        The result is an assessment of the submitted record—not a guarantee of truth, a certification of every source, or permission to act beyond the requesting system’s authority.

        Demonstrations

        An illustration is not a production record.

        Public-record illustrations explain the distinctions the protocol is designed to examine. They are not customer results or production-issued examinations.
