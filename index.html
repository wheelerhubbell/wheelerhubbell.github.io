Department II Advisory Practice & Epistemic Method

Decision Integrity

A disciplined framework for evaluating high-consequence judgment where certainty is unavailable and action cannot simply be postponed.

Separating Assertion from Authority

High-consequence decisions in jurisprudence, corporate governance, intelligence, and autonomous systems fail not from computational insufficiency, but from unexamined substitution—treating plausibility as authority, conflating assertions with verified standing, and stripping the essential boundary conditions that make claims true.

The Decision Integrity framework provides an auditable method to inspect what is known, isolate what is assumed, protect epistemic qualifiers, and ensure that authorization does not exceed admissible proof.

Department V The Imprint & Practice

Wheeler Hubbell & The House

Wheeler Hubbell is a writer, playwright, publisher, and researcher working across literary, dramatic, constitutional, and epistemic forms.

The work does not stay in one register. A book can ask a literary question. A play can place a disputed memory into the mouths of characters who cannot reconcile what happened. An essay can follow an observation to its natural conclusion. Research can demand a formal argument. Decision Integrity requires an executable method.

What unites the work is not a single subject, but the discipline of attention to the thing itself: what is present, what is absent, what can be claimed with warrant, and what follows from getting the distinction wrong.

Wheeler Hubbell Publishing is the independent publishing home of this work, responsible for design, typography, editorial curation, and the production of durable editions.
